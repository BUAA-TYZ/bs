# Fixed-Overlap Study

Each panel is a separate paired experiment; no scores are pooled across panels.
F30/F60/F120/F240 and J use equal case weights; J is the equal mean of the four age horizons. Restricted latency retains every arrived task, assigning undelivered tasks 14,400 seconds. Completed/total counts are pooled within each panel only.

Busy time and sent MB are sums across cases. Utilization is busy seconds divided by total available node-seconds over the full simulation (including drain), not only the arrival interval. Source/non-source identifies imaging roles, not the originating satellite of each individual task. ISL bytes include repeated-hop and result traffic; they are not a direct measure of raw-input offload. The raw-relay counter excludes ordinary distributed OFFLOAD transfers; zero raw_relay_hops does not mean zero raw-input offload. Executor-level traces are needed for exact task-locality. These are fixed-time-step measurements. Stable age-delivery scores do not establish convergence of short-task latency or communication volume; numerical sensitivity must be reported separately.
